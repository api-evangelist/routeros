It has come to our attention that a new way of brute force attack based on WPA2 standard using PMKID has come to light. This attack actually is a brute force attack on WPA2 preshared key. The reason this attack is considered effective is because it can be performed offline, without actually attempting to connect to AP, based on a single sniffed packet from a valid key exchange.
